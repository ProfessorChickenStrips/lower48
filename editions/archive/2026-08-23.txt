THE PLYMOUTH DETOUR THAT ISN'T

Route 3 passes Plymouth's front door, so the stop costs 45 minutes, not a detour. Plymouth Rock at Pilgrim Memorial State Park — free, waterfront, quick. Expectation management delivered with love: it is a modest boulder under a grand Greek portico, and that gap IS the charm. You two are collecting the story, not the geology. The Mayflower II beside it delivers the better photograph. Snap it, then go — the Green Mountains don't wait.
