Subject: The Morning Mile - Day 8 of 14: Provincetown

Good morning from West Yarmouth — you wake mid-Cape in the Uncommoner, two states claimed yesterday, one more waiting today across the same sand.

TODAY'S PLAN

Loop day: Chatham by 9:00 AM (≈30 minutes east), Wellfleet for oysters mid-day, Provincetown for the afternoon and Carnival week energy, then the 55-minute drive home to the same beds. Leave 8:25. No checkout, no packing — just the day bag and the camera.

WEATHER

Patchy rain nearby, high 68°F, southwest wind at 20 mph. It's August on the Atlantic — clouds blow through, sun follows. Rain plan: Commercial Street doesn't close for drizzle, and Mac's serves under a roof.

THE CHATHAM STOP

Fish Pier first: the morning boats unload, and the harbor seals gather below the ramp to steal bait. They bark, they argue, they steal from each other. Locals call it the show. Lighthouse after, then the village if you want it — but the seals are the real Chatham treasure, and they only perform in the morning.

MAC'S & THE OUTER CAPE

Wellfleet by midday: Mac's on the Pier for the oysters you came for, pulled from the flats you can see from the deck. Order a dozen, maybe two. The drive Wellfleet to Provincetown is 25 minutes of dune and scrub — the land gets thinner, the light gets bigger, and then Commercial Street appears at the end of the world.

PROVINCETOWN & CARNIVAL

You land during Carnival week — the town's 42nd annual festival, parades through Monday, the whole village dressed and dancing. It's theater, it's joy, it's unapologetically P-town. Stroll Commercial Street, find the Pilgrim Monument if you want the view, let the energy carry you. Dinner somewhere along the mile of restaurants — no reservation needed tonight; August Saturdays turn every kitchen into walk-in service.

CLAIM OF THE DAY

Massachusetts, the bonus — you already have it from 2020, but today earns the ritual anyway: a meal on Cape sand, a photo of the two of you with the Atlantic behind, and the Ledger stamp before bed. The 48 don't care about bonuses, but you do.

TONIGHT

Back to the Uncommoner by ~8:30–9:00, the drive home quiet after a long bright day. One sentence in the journal before sleep — tomorrow's edition reads it.

"I went to Provincetown once. It's the only sane place to be." — Tennessee Williams (who spent summers there in the 1940s and called it his favorite American town)
