THE CULTURE FILE

Cape May holds the nation's largest collection of Victorian homes — over 600 buildings, most from the 1870s–90s after an 1878 fire wiped the earlier resort town clean. The whole downtown is a National Historic Landmark District. Washington Street Mall, the pedestrian spine, was built in 1971 as one of America's first car-free shopping streets — a bold move for a beach town. And the Cape May diamonds you'll see in jewelry shops aren't diamonds at all: they're polished quartz pebbles, tumbled smooth by Delaware Bay waves, named by hopeful tourists in the 1800s who thought they'd struck it rich.
