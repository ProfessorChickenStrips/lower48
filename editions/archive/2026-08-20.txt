THE CULTURE FILE: Mystic gave Hollywood The Amistad trial (Spielberg shot courtroom scenes at the real building), gave pizza its first American temple (Pepe's, 1925, New Haven — 45 minutes west if the Oyster Club falls through), and gave literature its most famous whaling departure (Melville shipped from Mystic's docks in 1841 before writing Moby-Dick). Newport gave America its most outrageous summer: in 1895 the Duke of Marlborough arrived to marry Consuelo Vanderbilt at Marble House — dowry $2.5 million in gold, plus an annual income. The wedding made the front page of every paper in the country. The marriage lasted eleven years.
