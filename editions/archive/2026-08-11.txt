TASTE TUESDAY

Cúrate made its name long before the Biltmore Estate realized what Asheville was becoming. Chef Katie Button — a Michelin-starred toque from Spain, not Asheville by birth but Asheville by fierce adoption — opened the tapas bar in 2011 and turned it into the anchor of the city's dining renaissance. The jamón ibérico is carved tableside from legs hung along the wall. The patatas bravas arrive blistered and molten. The wine list runs deep into Riojas and Albariños you won't find at home. It's a 28-seat bar surrounded by a 48-seat dining room, walk-ins welcome at the bar around 5 PM most nights, reservations through Resy otherwise. If Cúrate's full on the 15th or 16th, her sister spot La Bodega by Cúrate pours the same sherries three doors down. Either one ends Day 1 or Day 2 the right way — and if both are slammed, Chai Pani's Indian street food two blocks over never disappoints.

THE POSTCARD

"There is something about the core of a volcano that enquires into your soul." — Anaïs Nin, who kept a house in the Blue Ridge and wrote some of her most introspective diaries there in the 1940s.

THE CULTURE FILE

Asheville owns two asymmetric superlatives: the largest privately-owned home in America (Biltmore, 178,926 square feet, 250 rooms) and one of the densest breweries-per-capita ratios in the country (nearly 30 taps within city limits). Thomas Wolfe was born here in 1900, wrote *Look Homeward, Angel* about the boarding house his mother ran on Spruce Street, and was essentially exiled by his neighbors for telling the truth too plainly — the book was banned from the Asheville public library for seven years. F. Scott Fitzgerald came here in 1936 during Zelda's Highland Hospital stays and wrote parts of his Hollywood crack-up essays while living at the Grove Park Inn. The Biltmore's pipe organ — a 1916 Skinner with 2,340 pipes — still plays during the Christmas season.

It's the full-access edition — upstairs bedrooms, the Dome Room, Jefferson's own genius and contradictions on full display — and it books out weeks ahead in summer. The 9:00 AM slot gets you done by 11 and on the road to Annapolis before lunch. Lock it tonight.
