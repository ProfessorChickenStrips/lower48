Subject: The Morning Mile - 8 days

Eight days. One week from this morning, you'll wake up in Asheville with the keys to a rental and 3,300 miles of unfinished business ahead of you.

FIELD-GUIDE FRIDAY: THE DELAWARE BAY CROSSING

The Cape May–Lewes Ferry isn't a sightseeing cruise — it's the only way to cross Delaware Bay by car without a 150-mile detour through Philadelphia. Eighty-five minutes, seventeen miles of open water, and a fair chance at dolphins riding the wake if you stand on the outer deck. But here's the field rule that matters: the 3:45 PM check-in cutoff on August 19 is absolute. Miss it and the crossing is forfeit, plus a $26 no-show fee. The drive from Annapolis is only two hours, but summer traffic on Route 50 can stretch that number — leave the Naval Academy by 11:30 and you'll have an hour of slack even after a Lewes lunch. Same photo IDs work all day: Gate 1 in the morning, ferry check-in in the afternoon. The vessel holds 100 cars and 320 passengers. You'll board from the first town in the first state and step off twenty minutes later in the oldest seaside resort in America. The car stays put; you don't.
