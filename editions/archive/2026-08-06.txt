THE CULTURE FILE

Acadia was the first national park created entirely from private donations — John D. Rockefeller Jr. alone gave more than 11,000 acres and built 57 miles of carriage roads by hand (no motors allowed, then or now). Édith Wharton spent childhood summers on Mount Desert Island; the granite and pine coast wound its way into her writing. And Peaks Island, your August 27 morning ferry ride, is where the Russwurm Family Historic House stands — the birthplace of John Brown Russwurm, co-founder of Freedom's Journal, the first African American-owned newspaper in the United States (1827).
