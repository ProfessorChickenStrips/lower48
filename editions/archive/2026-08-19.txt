THE CROSSING
The Cape May–Lewes Ferry has carried travelers across Delaware Bay since 1964. Seventeen miles of open water, 85 minutes of transit, and a legitimate chance of dolphins riding the bow wake. The MV Cape Henlopen and her sister ships are working vessels — no frills, just the crossing. Stand outside if the fog clears. The Delaware coastline slides into view around minute 60.

ONE DELIGHT
Second Street in Lewes: a six-block stretch of 18th-century homes, brick sidewalks, and the Cannonball House (hit by British naval fire in 1813, the shot still lodged in the foundation). It's the town William Penn's colonists named in 1631, two generations before Philadelphia. Walk it slowly — this is the oldest European settlement in Delaware, and it knows it.
