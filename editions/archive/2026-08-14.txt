THE CULTURE FILE — ASHEVILLE'S THREEFOLD CLAIM

Asheville punches far above its weight. First: Thomas Wolfe, who grew up here and wrote *Look Homeward, Angel* — his childhood boarding house still stands as a museum downtown. Second: the city sits at the exact intersection of the Blue Ridge and the Great Smokies, which is why George Vanderbilt chose this spot for his 250-room chateau in the 1890s. Third: Asheville has more breweries per capita than any American city except Portland, Oregon — the craft beer wave started here in 1994 when Highland Brewing opened in a basement. You're flying into a mountain town that built an empire, raised a novelist, and never stopped making things by hand.
